# Atlas 2030 — Experience & User Flows

Atlas is designed around **rhythms**, not sessions. Each rhythm has a strict time budget; the design must respect it.

## The four rhythms

| Rhythm | Frequency | Budget | Surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulse | Daily | ≤ 60 seconds | The "today" band of the Week screen |
| Review & Vision | Weekly | ~15 minutes, one sitting | Reflect screen |
| Season | Quarterly | ~45 minutes | Season ritual (guided flow) |
| Wander | Whenever | Unbounded, joyful | Atlas & Pillars (read-only browsing) |

**The week is the atomic unit of life in Atlas.** Systems run on weekly rhythms, the Question is weekly, review & vision are weekly. The home screen is therefore the *Week* — the day is just the row of it you're standing in. If daily use ever requires more than a minute, the design has failed.

---

## Flow 1 — First run: "The Founding" (once, ~30–45 min)

The onboarding is a ritual, not a form. Full-screen, one question at a time, serif type, no chrome:

1. **The letter.** Write a short letter to your 2030 self. Atlas seals it (visibly encrypted/locked) and schedules it to open in December 2030. This is the app's emotional anchor.
2. **The six statements.** For each pillar, write the identity statement (with good example placeholders as scaffolding).
3. **The systems.** Declare the recurring engines already running in your life (Deep Learning, Engineering Lab, University, English, Reading, Gym, Basketball…) with their weekly rhythms. This is recognition, not planning — Atlas starts by acknowledging the life you already lead.
4. **The first season.** Name it. Choose 2–3 focus pillars. Write 1–3 intentions.
5. **The curiosity lane.** Ask this week's Question. Add the book you're currently reading, with its *why* and where you are in it.
6. **First pulse.** Do today's 60-second check-in immediately, so day one already has texture.

Exit onboarding directly onto a Today screen that already feels alive.

## Flow 2 — Daily pulse (≤ 60 seconds)

1. Open app → **Week**. Header shows the season and the week ("Foundations · week 4 of 12"); the curiosity band shows the live Question and current book; the system rows show the week's rhythm filling in. Today's column is focused.
2. Mark today's system sessions — one tap each, optional one-line session note. No dialogs.
3. Energy dial — one drag or tap.
4. Optional one-liner. Placeholder rotates through gentle prompts ("What mattered today?").
5. Done-state: the screen settles — texture updates, a quiet line like "Day 847 of becoming." No fireworks.

**Capture anytime:** ⌘K from anywhere → a milestone, a book idea that just changed your thinking, a note toward the Question, a session mark — filed correctly without navigating. This is the Raycast/Linear DNA and the single most important interaction in the app: *capture must never require navigation.* Idea captures from books are the canonical ⌘K use case: you're reading, something lands, ten seconds later it's in Atlas and you're back in the book.

## Flow 3 — Weekly Review & Vision (~15 min, one sitting, e.g. Sunday)

Two movements, one ceremony. This replaces — and *is* — the "Weekly Review" and "Weekly Vision" from your current life; Atlas hosts them rather than tracking them.

**Movement I — Review (look back):**
1. **The week played back:** system rows filled against their rhythms, the Question and its state, book progress and captured ideas, milestones, the energy line.
2. Three prompts: *What moved? What drained me? What did I learn?*

**Movement II — Vision (look forward):**
3. **The Question:** carry the current one forward (no penalty — deep questions span weeks) or close it with a write-up and ask the next.
4. **The systems, glanced:** pause one, adjust a rhythm — small course corrections belong here, not in daily flow.
5. One optional intention line for the week.
6. Close: Atlas surfaces one memory ("Four weeks ago you wrote: …").

Skipping a week is allowed and undramatic. Two skipped weeks in a row → the Week screen adds one soft line ("It's been a while since you looked back") — that is the maximum nagging the app will ever do.

## Flow 4 — Season ritual (4×/year, ~45 min, guided)

The crown jewel. A deliberate, full-screen, multi-step ceremony:

1. **Unsealing.** The note-to-self from the season's start is opened and shown first.
2. **The season played back.** Milestones, system density arcs, the questions asked and answered, the books finished (and their best ideas), energy over the 12 weeks, best excerpts from weekly reflections (auto-surfaced, editable selection).
3. **Honest grading.** Each intention: `became / moved / didn't move` + one sentence. The language is trajectory language — "didn't move" is information, not failure.
4. **Identity check.** Each focus pillar's identity statement is shown: *Still true? Still yours?* Revise if needed (versioned).
5. **Composition.** Name the next season, pick focus pillars, write intentions, confirm or adjust the system roster (with the same "start smaller" resistance), write and seal the new note-to-self.
6. **The closing view.** The Atlas timeline zooms out one level, the finished season slots into the year, and for a moment you see the whole journey. This is the screenshot-your-life moment; it should be the most beautiful render in the app.

## Flow 5 — Wandering (read-only, anytime)

Browsing the Atlas view, a pillar's history, old reflections. No inputs required, nothing tracked, no "engagement." Design intent: this should feel like flipping through a beautifully typeset book about someone you're proud of.

## Edge experiences (designed, not accidental)

- **The bad week / the vanishing.** You disappear for 3 weeks. On return: no guilt wall, no "you missed 21 days." The Week screen simply says "Welcome back" and shows the season, the current week, the standing Question, the open book. Density metrics have naturally dimmed — that's the only trace. There is an optional one-tap "life happened" annotation that marks the gap on the timeline so future-you understands it (illness, exams, travel) instead of reading it as failure.
- **The abandoned season.** A season can be ended early or rewritten mid-flight via a lightweight version of the ritual. Life > plan; the app must never make the plan feel more real than life.
- **The empty day.** Nothing checked, nothing written: the app remains beautiful and silent. Tomorrow is simply tomorrow.
